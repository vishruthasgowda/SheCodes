FAQ

Questions, answered

Is DocuClear legal advice?

No. It explains documents in simple language and highlights things to look at. For important decisions, consult a qualified lawyer. Medical documents are explained for understanding only, never diagnosis.

Is my data safe?

Documents are private to your account, and you can mask Aadhaar, PAN, bank and contact details before analysis.

Which files can I upload?

PDFs, photos/scans (JPG, PNG) and text files.

Can I compare two documents?

Yes. Cross-document checks catch mismatches, such as an offer letter that says ₹8 LPA while the agreement says ₹6.5 LPA.
